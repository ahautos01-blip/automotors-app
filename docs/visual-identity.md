# AUTOMOTORS visual identity

Maintain the approved light editorial identity across discovery, listings, profiles and publication. Recognition comes from consistent execution; the interface does not claim national leadership or invent inventory, reviews, verification or selling outcomes.

## Foundations

- Deep green (`marketInk`) for primary actions and principal text; warm paper backgrounds; white surfaces; muted readable secondary text.
- Gold is a restrained accent for small brand rules, highlighted benefits and selected details, never low-contrast body text on white.
- Use theme typography and icon sizing. One primary action per decision; consistent rounded surfaces, 48px minimum interaction targets and 8/12/16/24 spacing.
- Respect text scaling and reduced-motion preferences. The reveal animation is short and disabled when the system requests reduced motion.

## Discovery

Show all four categories first, then a compact heading and search. Use real listing photos in the inventory rather than an unrelated cover photograph. Saved searches, favourites and plan access remain available. Distinguish a loading catalogue, a failed request, no results for filters and an empty launch catalogue. An empty catalogue invites publication or a saved search without fabricated listings or claims about audience size.

## Listing

Photos first, then vehicle identity and advertised price, seller, main specifications and optional details. Secondary feed photos use a landscape ratio and stack above the text on narrow screens or with large text. Favourite, compare and detail actions remain independent. Technical information is labelled as seller-provided. Seller photos fall back gracefully; the seller entry opens their listings and reviews. Existing report/block and purchase safety functions remain accessible.

## Profile

Use a consistent cover/avatar composition for both professional and personal accounts. A decorative green fallback is not a photograph or a verification indicator. Centre the avatar across the lower edge of the cover. Render the name once; professional accounts display their registered business name. Keep actual locality, member-since information, real reviews and counts. Professional status is declared by the user, with its existing explanation on tap. Do not introduce an identity-verified or vehicle-inspected badge.

## Publication

Preserve build 53's visible four-plan comparison, persistent selected-plan purchase action and secondary free-publication option. The vehicle/photos/review flow retains drafts, optional technical details, catalogues and the existing store verification process.

## Rollout

Inspect actual widget previews and exercise discovery, detail contacts, profiles, reporting, favourites, purchase entry, drafts and large-text layouts. Real inventory, seller acquisition and promotion remain separate business work; visual changes alone do not guarantee national recognition.
